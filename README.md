# AI, Mental Wealth, and UK Public Policy

## Overview
This project critically evaluates how the UK government should promote and regulate artificial intelligence to support mental wealth, with a focus on NHS mental health services.

## Objectives
- Assess the benefits of AI in mental health care
- Identify ethical, clinical, and regulatory risks
- Evaluate current UK government initiatives
- Propose balanced policy recommendations

## Key Themes
- AI-driven triage and diagnostic tools
- Patient safety and algorithmic bias
- Data privacy and governance
- Public trust and accountability

## Approach
- Critical literature review
- Policy analysis
- Evaluation of real-world NHS AI implementations

## Case Study
- Limbic Access and AI triage systems in NHS mental health services

## Conclusions
- AI can significantly enhance access and efficiency
- Strong regulation is essential to mitigate harm
- Government must balance innovation with public protection

## Structure
- `report/` – final academic essay
- `references/` – key sources and policy documents
